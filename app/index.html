Four phases, in order. The only trophies you can truly miss are in Phase 2 — do them before the final mission.

    Play the story

    Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the named story missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). All unmissable — just play. 0 / 12 story trophies.

  3. 3

    Strangers, activities & side content

    Finish the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the 8-strand set, races, shooting range, drone races, fishing, arcade, meditation, hunting and the rest. 0 / 24 side trophies.

    Cleanup — collectibles & 100%

    Card sets, WAiNK locations, meditation spots, 100 vehicles, garage properties, laundered bills and the 50 challenges. Finish with A Long Road (100% completion) and the Platinum pops. 0 / 5 collectible trophies.
